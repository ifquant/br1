**Summary:**
This book examines the trade-offs between time and money, arguing that people often make poor decisions by prioritizing money over time. It emphasizes that "money is time" rather than "time is money," and aims to help readers reshape their lifestyle choices through behavioral insights and data analysis.

**Key Insights:**
1. People systematically undervalue time in favor of money, leading to long-term costs in health and happiness.
2. There is no universal "right" choice between time and money—optimal decisions depend on individual circumstances and life stages.
3. Society and policymakers need to better recognize the value of time, as time scarcity affects everyone regardless of economic status.
